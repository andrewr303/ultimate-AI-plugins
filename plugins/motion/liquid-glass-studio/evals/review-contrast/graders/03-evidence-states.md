The final review gives its checks explicit evidence states, such as checked, not checked and not applicable (or measured, static and inferred), and any contrast ratio it reports either came from a measurement the agent actually ran in the trace (a script, browser or tool output) or is labelled as an estimate or not measured. It does not present an unmeasured ratio as measured, and it does not report a check as passed when it was not run.
