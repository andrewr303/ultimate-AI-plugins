Spring tides and the new pontoon

The harbour board finished the north pontoon on Tuesday, just in time for the highest tides of the year. Owners on the outer moorings can now step ashore without the dinghy shuffle, and the fuel berth has moved to the end of the new arm.

Expect a strong ebb through the entrance on Friday and Saturday mornings. The board asks skippers to keep clear of the dredger working the channel between eight and noon.
