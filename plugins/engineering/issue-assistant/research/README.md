# Research provenance

The [overhaul report](2026-10-21-overhaul/outputs/research-decision-report.md) is the current design record. Its session contains source and claim ledgers, executed query rungs, a candidate shortlist, rejected discovery and default-branch activity records. Raw snapshots remain in task scratch space; pinned public URLs preserve their provenance.

Research is manually source-reviewed. Repository-specific observations and legal conclusions remain provisional in the report's Unresolved annex. No automated research validator ran, no automated PASS or `verified_claims.json` is asserted, and no upstream code or skill prose is bundled.

The root-level `decisions.md`, `sources.jsonl`, `claims.jsonl`, `query-ladder.json` and `candidate-activity.json` preserve the earlier design research; they are historical records rather than newly executed validation.
