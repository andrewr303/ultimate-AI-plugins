## How the sources were used

- Eight source archives were supplied as read-only inputs (folders named `<project>-main`). They were read as untrusted data. Nothing in them was executed, installed or imported.
- Adapted material was rewritten in our own words. Where it states something about a search engine or AI system, SEO-GEO-god cites a dated fact in [references/current-facts.md](references/current-facts.md) or marks the statement "needs verification". Numbers taken over as rules of thumb are labelled heuristics.
- Seven sources contributed adapted material. Their license texts ship verbatim in `licenses/`, byte-identical to each archive's `LICENSE` file.
- GEOFlow was consulted for ideas only, so no GEOFlow license file is shipped (see [GEOFlow: consulted for ideas only](#geoflow-consulted-for-ideas-only)).

## GEOFlow: consulted for ideas only

GEOFlow 3.2.0-beta.2 (AGPL-3.0-only, Copyright 2026 Yao Jingang) was consulted for ideas only. No GEOFlow code, text, prompts, regexes, schemas, config literals, thresholds or weights were copied or closely paraphrased. Each idea below was designed again in our own words, with our own structure and our own numbers. Because nothing from GEOFlow is redistributed, SEO-GEO-god ships no GEOFlow license file.

Ideas consulted:

- A URL and index-state matrix and slug governance for migrations (`references/migrations.md`).
- Structured-data integrity: list markup must match the visible items, event markup only for one real event, internal scores never presented as ratings (`references/structured-data-matrix.md`, `skills/seo-structured-data`).
- A regression guard for template edits, and reports tied to the content and rules versions they used (`references/ci-recipes.md`, `skills/seo-ci-guard`).
- Measurement stages, counting a brand as cited separately from merely mentioned, separate results per AI surface, and "no computable result" instead of zero (`references/measurement-protocol.md`).
- Drafting an llms.txt that respects each page's indexing state (`references/agent-readiness.md`).
- Root-cause deduplication of findings and keeping scores separate from gates (`references/scoring-and-prioritization.md`).

Not used from GEOFlow, besides all of its code and text: link pools, mass article and title production, multi-subdomain content farms, robots blocks aimed at image and social-preview bots, and meta keywords tags.

## Material deliberately not included

- **claude-seo `skills/seo-flow/`**: the FLOW framework and prompt library, which that archive marks "Framework and prompts © Daniel Agrici, CC BY 4.0". Not included, so its separate license does not apply to this plugin.
- **eGEOagents `SUBSTRATE.md`** and its lint: the archive states that this file is vendored verbatim from another project (mverab/loopstack), whose license is not in the archive. Not included; the SEO-GEO-god ledger is a separate, simpler design.
- **aaron-marketing-skills CORE-EEAT and CITE item texts**: the archive marks these as adapted from or based on separate upstream repositories (https://github.com/aaron-he-zhu/core-eeat-content-benchmark and https://github.com/aaron-he-zhu/cite-domain-rating, as stated in the archive), whose licenses were not verified. No item texts, tables, weights or scorer are included. CORE-EEAT is credited as a concept in `references/content-quality-rubrics.md`, and the checklists there are our own.
- **notfair-plugin references copied from aaron-marketing-skills**: some notfair-plugin references are copies or expansions of aaron-marketing-skills files; for example, its keyword-research `example-report.md` and schema-markup-generator `schema-decision-tree.md` are byte-identical to the aaron-marketing-skills originals. Material of that origin is credited to aaron-marketing-skills and handled under Apache-2.0, never credited to notfair-plugin. Neither of those two files is reproduced here.

## platform-motion-wizard (same repository)

platform-motion-wizard is another plugin in the same repository as SEO-GEO-god (version 1.1.0 per its `plugin.json`), not one of the eight external sources, and it has no license file of its own. SEO-GEO-god follows its structure in these places:
