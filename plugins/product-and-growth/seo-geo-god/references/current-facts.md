# Current facts registry

This file is the single source of dated facts about search engines, AI systems, crawlers and web standards that SEO-GEO-god skills, references, rules and reports rely on. Every claim about how Google Search, Chrome Lighthouse, the Gemini app, Bing and Copilot, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Claude or another system behaves must cite a fact ID from this file, or be marked "needs verification". Numbers that are our own thresholds are labelled "heuristic" where they are used and do not appear here as facts.

As of 2026-11-01. Facts were checked against the listed primary sources on 2026-11-01 unless the label says otherwise.

## How to read this file

Each fact row has five columns: the ID (with an anchor such as `f-t-18`, so skills link to `current-facts.md#f-t-18`), the fact in one sentence in our own words, the evidence label, the page's own date or the as-of date, and the exact source URL. "URL to confirm" means the vendor page was identified but its exact address was not confirmed in the research pass.

| Label | Meaning | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V | Vendor primary source (the company's own docs, blog or help page) | State as current for that vendor and product |
| S | Standards body primary source (IETF Datatracker, W3C Community Group report) | State the document status (draft, not an RFC) with the fact |
| R | Regulator primary source | Quote the obligation, not a predicted implementation |
| P | Peer-reviewed paper | Quote numbers with the study's setup and caveats |
| PP | Preprint, not peer reviewed | Say "preprint" whenever it is cited |
| IR | Independent research organization, not peer reviewed | Say who ran it and when the data was collected |
| 3P | Third-party source only | Write "needs verification"; never present as fact |
| SR | Primary page seen only in search-result snippets (fetch was blocked) | Write "needs verification" next to the claim |

Rules for citing:

- Cite the narrowest fact that supports the sentence. If a sentence needs two facts, cite both.
- Do not extend a fact beyond its wording. F-AI-06 says Google-Extended does not affect Google Search inclusion; it does not say anything about other vendors.
- Say which product a fact is about. Google Search ignoring llms.txt (F-AI-11) and Chrome Lighthouse checking for it (F-AI-27) are different products and both are true.
- A fact needed by a skill but missing here goes into the reference that needs it, with its source URL and label, and is listed for an ID in the next registry update.

## How to update

1. Re-open the primary URL in the row. Do not update from a third-party summary.
2. Change the fact text, the page date and the label together. Keep the ID; never reuse an ID for a different fact.
3. Update the matching data files (`data/*.json` `as_of`, `data/ai-crawlers.json`, `data/structured-data.json`, `data/thresholds.json`) and the tests that pin them in the same change.
4. Move items between the fact tables and the "Not verified" list when their evidence changes.
5. Run `SG doctor` (defined in each skill as `python3 -I <plugin-root>/scripts/seo_geo.py`) to confirm data freshness, then the plugin's unit tests.
6. Record the review date in the "As of" line at the top of this file.

## Not verified / third-party only
<a id="not-verified-third-party-only"></a>

Do not present these as facts. If a user or source repeats one, say it needs verification and point to what is confirmed above.

- **OpenAI help pages.** The Publishers and Developers FAQ, the ChatGPT search help article and OpenAI's 2026-10-30 post on training preferences returned HTTP 403 to the research fetcher. The `utm_source=chatgpt.com` tagging ([F-AI-03](#f-ai-03)) and OpenAI's support for the shared training token are known only from snippets and trade press; OpenAI's implementation date is unknown.
- **ChatGPT agent identity.** Reports that ChatGPT agent signs requests with `Signature-Agent: "https://chatgpt.com"` come from third parties, not from OpenAI's bots page.
- **Bing Webmaster Guidelines rewrite.** Reports (February 2026) that Bing's guidelines now name GEO, add a prompt-injection section and expand keyword stuffing to "artificially engineered language", and that NOARCHIVE/NOCACHE currently limit Copilot grounding, are third-party. The Bing help pages render client-side and could not be read. Bing's crawl-delay and rel=next/prev handling were not verified.
- **Bing Webmaster API quotas.** Only 2019 figures were found (adaptive up to 10,000 URLs per day, 500 per batch call).
- **ByteDance and Cohere crawlers.** No vendor documentation was found for Bytespider, `cohere-ai` or `cohere-training-data-crawler`; their purposes and robots.txt compliance are third-party and conflicting.
- **GA4 AI Assistant channel.** The launch date (reported as about 2026-05-13) and whether Perplexity and Claude are on Google's internal assistant list are third-party only.
- **AI referrer hosts per app.** The product domains are known, but which referrer each assistant, mobile app or AI browser actually sends is third-party reporting; many AI sessions arrive with no referrer at all.
- **Search Console generative-AI report limits.** Claims that the report has no click or query data, starts on 2026-05-18 or carries a "Beta" label are third-party only.
- **UK CMA granularity.** Reported page- or directory-level controls by December 2026 or March 2027 are third-party; the gov.uk release states only "nine months".
- **Cloudflare "Pay Per Use".** A reported pivot from Pay Per Crawl is third-party; Cloudflare's docs still say private beta. Whether the 2025-07-01 default block applied only to new domains is also third-party.
- **llms-full.txt.** It is a community and vendor convention, not part of the llms.txt v2 page text; the llmstxt.org "Changes" page was not read. Earlier remarks by Google staff about llms.txt are superseded by Google's 2026-06-15 documentation ([F-AI-11](#f-ai-11)).
- **AIPREF history.** Earlier vocabulary labels from 2025 drafts were not re-checked; only the `-08` labels in [F-AI-16](#f-ai-16) are confirmed.
- **WebMCP trial end.** The origin-trial end (about Chrome 156), the deprecation of the `navigator.modelContext` alias and the Lighthouse version that shipped Agentic Browsing are third-party only.
- **NLWeb endpoints.** The `/ask` and `/mcp` endpoint paths and adoption figures are third-party; the repository confirms only the MCP `ask` method.
- **AutoGEO and later GEO preprints.** AutoGEO's peer-review status is unknown, and follow-up preprints that report it below baseline were seen only as snippets.
- **Vendor GEO, citation and CTR studies.** Studies from SEO tool vendors and agencies were not verified. Label any of them "vendor study", give the date, and never present them as causal.
- **Perplexity "stealth crawling" dispute.** Cloudflare's August 2025 accusation and Perplexity's denial are known through third-party coverage of both sides. Do not assert either side as fact.
- **FAQ tooling timeline.** Removal of the FAQ report, filter and Rich Results Test support (June 2026) and Search Console API support (August 2026), and the claim that existing FAQPage markup need not be removed, are third-party only. Google confirms only [F-T-02](#f-t-02).
- **CrUX ad metrics date.** The CrUX API page lists experimental ad metrics, but their launch date (reported as 2026-09-15) is third-party.
- **Search Console soft-navigation switch.** A possible move of Search Console Core Web Vitals to soft-navigation metrics after a May 2027 graduation is third-party.
- **Open Graph and X (Twitter) tags as ranking signals.** No Google primary statement says they are or are not ranking signals. Google documents `og:title`, `og:image` and `og:site_name` only as display inputs. Write "no primary statement".
- **"About 600 px" title truncation.** No primary source gives a pixel width; Google says only that titles are truncated to fit the device width ([F-T-38](#f-t-38)). Treat any fixed pixel or character limit as folklore.
- **Google Business Profile 2026 changes.** Public Q&A removal from profiles, "Ask Maps", an April 2026 review policy on staff names and kiosks, WhatsApp messaging and Offers merged into Updates are third-party and partly conflicting. Only the Q&A API discontinuation ([F-T-47](#f-t-47)) is Google-confirmed.
- **"Inside Googlebot" post.** The March 2026 post body could not be read; the 2 MB limit is confirmed on the Googlebot page instead ([F-T-19](#f-t-19)). That other Google crawlers keep a 15 MB default is third-party.
- **Dataset Search wording.** That Dataset markup is "Dataset Search only" comes from the changelog summary; the Dataset page frames the markup around Dataset Search without saying so explicitly.
- **Video ranking note.** A 2025-06-04 changelog summary saying sites blocking Googlebot from video files may see ranking drops does not appear on the video page itself.
- **FID end of support.** INP replaced FID in 2024; the further claim that Chrome ended FID support in September 2024 was seen in web.dev snippets only ([F-T-14](#f-t-14)).
- **Search Analytics hourly window.** The "about 10 days of hourly data" figure comes from third parties quoting Google.
- **Mobile-first completion post.** The June 2024 post was seen only as a snippet and a guessed URL returned 404 ([F-T-28](#f-t-28)).
- **Faceted navigation document origin.** The original publication date (believed December 2024) was not confirmed; the page shows 2025-12-18.
- **Sitemap ping endpoint and data retention.** The 2023 retirement of the sitemap ping endpoint and the 16-month Search Console data retention are long-standing but were not re-verified in this pass.
