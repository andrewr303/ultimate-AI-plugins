## Skill catalog

- [marketing-page-studio](../skills/marketing-page-studio/SKILL.md): Use when building or redesigning a product homepage, feature page or marketing website around accurate interfaces, meaningful interactive demonstrations and conversion. Also use for a complete product-led scroll storytelling site; do not activate for unrelated application bugs or standalone video exports.
- [mps-browser-review](../skills/mps-browser-review/SKILL.md): Use when reviewing and repairing the actual browser rendering, interactions, responsiveness, accessibility, performance or reduced-motion behavior of a product marketing site.
- [mps-composition](../skills/mps-composition/SKILL.md): Use when implementing a product homepage layout or presenting accurate screenshots through browser frames, detail crops, mobile compositions, layered surfaces or responsive page sections.
- [mps-conversion-story](../skills/mps-conversion-story/SKILL.md): Use when writing accurate benefit and CTA copy or extending a product homepage into coherent feature, workflow, use-case, comparison or pricing pages.
- [mps-delivery](../skills/mps-delivery/SKILL.md): Use when packaging a completed product marketing site with runnable source, preview commands, optimized assets, QA evidence and a self-contained capture or video handoff.
- [mps-direction](../skills/mps-direction/SKILL.md): Use when choosing the audience, product promise, proof, objections, CTA or narrative for a product marketing page before composing it.
- [mps-interactive-surfaces](../skills/mps-interactive-surfaces/SKILL.md): Use when building bounded interactive product previews, deterministic AI demonstrations, editable results, search/filter, reordering, before/after or replayable workflows inside a marketing page.
- [mps-product-evidence](../skills/mps-product-evidence/SKILL.md): Use when discovering a product from a repository, app, website, screenshots or documentation, capturing its states, and building evidence-backed assets for marketing pages.
- [mps-scroll-story](../skills/mps-scroll-story/SKILL.md): Use when a product workflow should change with forward and reverse scroll, including pinned stages, synchronized interface states, progress controls and a reduced-motion reading alternative.
