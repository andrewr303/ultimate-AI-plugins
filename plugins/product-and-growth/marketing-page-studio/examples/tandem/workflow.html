THE WORKFLOW / REQUEST REVIEW

Keep the context.
Clarify the handoff.

A support request should travel with the information that makes it useful. Here is how this sample product keeps the original message, the suggested owner, and the reply together.

Try it with sample data

THE ORIGINAL REQUEST

Start with the
customer’s words.

Choose a message from the synthetic inbox or write your own. The local fixture recognizes billing and access examples. Other messages receive a general-support response.

Requests never leave your browser. Names and messages are sample data.

SAMPLE / INTAKE
“Could someone check the two payments on my account? I only placed one order.”

MAKE OWNERSHIP VISIBLE

A category.
A next owner.

A deterministic rule maps the message to a sample category, priority, and owner. Billing goes to Alex; access goes to Sam. This illustrates a route rather than calling a real AI model.

The suggested reply stays attached to the same request.

YOU MAKE THE CALL

Suggestions are
ready to edit.

Edit the draft in place and mark it ready for review. The example records that state locally in memory. It never sends a message, changes an account, or makes a refund.

Reset clears your changes and restores the starting message.

WHAT THE FIXTURE PROVES

A working interaction.
A clearly bounded promise.

Implemented here
Local search, rule-based classification, draft editing, queue reordering, direct and scroll-driven workflow previews, explicit empty/error/retry/reset states.

Outside this example
Live AI, real customer records, team accounts, persistence, integrations, and outbound sending. None of these are implied by the sample interface.
