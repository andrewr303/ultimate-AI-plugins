A FEW CLEAR ANSWERS

Know what
you’re trying.

Is this a real service?

This is an implemented, local product fixture built to demonstrate the studio plugins. Its visible interactions work, but it has no live AI, authentication, payment processing, or outbound messaging.

Where does the sample data go?

Nowhere. The demonstration runs in your browser and does not call an external service. Reset restores the supplied synthetic data.

What can I try?

Search requests, choose a sample case, organize a request, edit a reply, mark it ready, reorder the review queue, and reset. The failure case lets you test retry and cancellation.

Can I use it without animation?

Yes. With reduced motion enabled, the workflow remains readable as separate states, and every interaction has a direct control. The main copy and navigation also work with JavaScript disabled.
