# Conversion copy and page systems

Read [copy and pages](../../references/copy-and-pages.md) and use claim IDs from the evidence ledger.

1. Translate each supported capability into a user outcome and pair it with visible product behavior. Keep headings specific; remove unsupported superlatives and vague speed/security promises.
2. Give the homepage one clear primary conversion action. Secondary links should answer a useful question without competing with it. Name the action the destination actually performs.
3. Give feature/workflow pages their own buyer question, causal demonstration, limitations and next step. Reuse navigation and tokens, not a repeated homepage skeleton.
4. For use cases, show realistic scenario input and outcome, clearly identifying sample data. Comparisons require current comparable facts and sources. Pricing requires supplied or verified prices, units, billing conditions and destinations.
5. Keep product labels consistent across UI, screenshots, narration and copy. Write useful alternatives and captions that explain important content without duplicating every visible label.
6. Test links and text in the browser. At mobile widths, check headline wraps, unbroken terms, form labels, validation and CTA clarity.

Do not add fake testimonial or logo rows to fill a section. When evidence is missing, replace a claim with a bounded demonstrable statement or omit it. Produce a copy/claim map so later edits remain traceable.
