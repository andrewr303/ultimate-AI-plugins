# Angle library

Pick the one point the cover letter leads with. These are starting points, not templates; write every opening fresh from the actual listing. Supporting proof must have status **Use** in `../../../references/andrew-profile.md`, worded as that file's proof library writes it. A **Confirm** item appears only as its placeholder. The ROI calculator's tense is a Confirm item: keep the noun phrase and write `[[CONFIRM: built or building]]` wherever a sentence needs "I built" or "I'm building".

| --- | --- | --- |

## Calibration (fictional listing, not Andrew's history)

The example below is deliberately a job type Andrew rarely bids on, so it shows the move without handing him a ready-made opening for his core work. For a made-up listing asking someone to fix a WordPress booking plugin that started double-charging customers after an update:

> Weak: "Hi, I'm an experienced developer with strong WordPress and PHP skills, and I'm confident I'm the perfect fit for your project."

> Stronger: "Hi Dana, double charges right after an update usually point to a payment hook now firing twice, so I'd start by reproducing one booking on a staging copy and logging each call before touching the live site."

The stronger version names the client's real risk, states an approach, and gives a reason to keep reading. Do not reuse its wording or its sentence shape; build each opening from the listing in front of you.
