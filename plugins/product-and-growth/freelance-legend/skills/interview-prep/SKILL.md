# Interview and call prep

Read `<data-dir>/profile.md`, `../../references/voice.md`, and the proposal package or listing for this job. Keep every answer inside **Use** claims; availability, start date, weekly hours, and any rate change are `[[CONFIRM]]` items until the freelancer confirms them.

## Before the call

Produce a one-page brief:

1. **Goal of the call:** what the freelancer needs to leave with (confirmed scope, decision maker, budget fit, start date, access to data or code).
2. **Thirty-second intro:** problem-first, then the one or two proof points that matter for this client. No résumé recital.
3. **Agenda (15 to 30 minutes):** their goal and current workflow; data and systems; what done looks like; timeline and decision process; next step.
4. **Questions that change scope or price:** for example, who owns the source model, which numbers must reconcile, how outputs are reviewed, what is already built, who approves milestones, and which environments he gets access to.
5. **Likely questions with truthful answers:** "Have you done this exact thing?", "Why should we pick you?", "Can you start Monday?", "Can you lower the rate?", "Do you have a team?". Honest "not yet" answers pair with the closest supported experience.
6. **Rate and terms stance:** the number from `pricing-scoping`, the floor, and what can trade (scope, phasing, milestone order) instead of rate.
7. **Red flags to listen for:** requests to move off-platform, unpaid test work, payment outside the platform, vague ownership of deliverables (see `job-vetting`).

Calls happen through the platform's own tools before a contract, per `../../references/platform-rules.md`. Do not propose phone, personal video links, or messaging apps unless the platform allows it at that stage.

## Recorded video answers

If the platform asks for a recorded answer instead of a cover letter, write five or six short talking points in this order (problem, proof, approach, first milestone, one question) plus an optional written note. Keep it speakable: short sentences, no jargon stacks, about 60 to 90 seconds.

## After the call

Draft a recap message for the platform's messages: what was agreed, open questions with owners, the proposed next step, and any scope or price change as a clear proposal (hand pricing back to `pricing-scoping` if anything moved). No contact details before a contract.
