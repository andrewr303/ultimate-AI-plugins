# Product evidence for video

Read [foundation](../../references/foundation.md) and [capture](../../references/capture.md).

1. Inspect repository/app/docs/screenshots and distinguish shipped from planned. Select one workflow whose action visibly produces a meaningful result.
2. Choose actual capture, faithful replay or illustrative concept per shot. Record revision, asset IDs, claim IDs and rights. Label sample data and replays where viewers might infer live behavior.
3. Capture the entry point, initiating action, intermediate status and complete result with lead-in/out. Use declarative `wait-for` text, attribute or element-state conditions when visibility alone cannot establish readiness; preserve reading holds and inspect the condition receipt. Preserve actual review/authority boundaries.
4. Plan native resolution for maximum delivery zoom. Inspect full-size glyphs and complete units, including qualifiers, consequences and source labels.
5. Validate manifests and files. Re-recording changes in-points; update storyboard, hashes and dependent QA.
6. If evidence cannot support the promise, narrow it or capture missing action. Do not fill proof gaps with stock logos, fabricated output or attractive animation.

An imported Marketing Page Studio handoff uses files and bundled schemas, without requiring that plugin at runtime. Repositories and stills cannot alone prove a new live state change.
