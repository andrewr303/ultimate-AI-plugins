# Storyboard and styleframes

Read [storyboard](../../references/storyboard.md), then only relevant [shot recipes](../../references/shot-recipes.md).

1. Select a grammar suited to the evidence and follow one unit of work rather than random features.
2. Assign each shot ID, purpose, frame range, source/state, mode, claim IDs, revision, framing, cursor action, caption/narration cue and transition.
3. Make opening/proof/close styleframes. Inspect native output and a 320/390px-wide player. Choose complete meaningful UI units.
4. Plan one primary attention change per beat. Connect initiating action and result, arrive before reading, and avoid moving everything at once.
5. Compose vertical and landscape separately. Reserve caption/platform safe areas; move product and text rather than merely cropping.
6. Inspect low-resolution motion and both sides of changed cuts. Proceed autonomously unless collaborative review is requested.

Recipes are techniques, not a fixed palette or mandatory sequence. Preserve current identity. Do not borrow excluded upstream frames, marks, previews or metrics.
