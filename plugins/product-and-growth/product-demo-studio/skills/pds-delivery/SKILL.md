# Product video delivery

Read [delivery](../../references/delivery.md). Complete authorized production and repair.

1. Include finals in requested placements/ratios, editable scene/job/storyboard, posters, captions, copy and experiment manifest.
2. Preserve brand/evidence/capture/rights/export objects, revision, source/configuration hashes and artifact-bound QA. Keep modes explicit.
3. Reproduce a selected render from a clean packaged copy and verify every final plus linked poster/captions. Never substitute an old preview.
4. Create a self-contained website handoff through the shared command; the consuming page does not need this plugin. Include a silent loop only where requested/appropriate.
5. Name material limits: host routing, platform-spec verification, optional adapters, audio listening and devices. Keep scopes distinct.
6. Link finals and detailed evidence concisely. Publishing, posting, spend and third-party uploads require requested scope.

Remove auth/private data/caches and excluded source media. Preserve notices and rights. Synthetic scenarios are not customer traction or measured outcomes.
