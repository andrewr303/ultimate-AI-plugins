# Hooks and causal scripts

## Start from proof

Inspect the strongest source at delivery size. Build the shortest causal path to it. If a shot has only an edited result, capture its initiating action or describe it as a result view. A typical short path is recognizable context, input/action, resulting state, bounded benefit and CTA. A result-first hook still needs honest causal explanation.

| Hook approach | Function | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct workflow | Action creates interest immediately | Visible input/action/result |
| Problem recognition | Names an audience situation | Product resolves that stated part |
| Before/after | Makes change inspectable | Comparable sourced states |
| Control/trust | Answers automation concern | Accurate review/edit boundary |
| Feature reveal | Introduces shipped behavior | Current revision and feature evidence |

Interest within 1–2 seconds and early proof are editable social defaults. They are not retention promises. Avoid an opening spent entirely on unsupported logo animation.

## Picture and words

Use a table of frame/time, action, onscreen line, narration, claim IDs and source/state. Each sentence points to something specific. Keep labels consistent and avoid long lists competing with motion.

Use actual narration duration/alignment. A locked recording outranks an old text draft; do not stretch or accelerate it silently. Reveal the relevant detail near the corresponding phrase and preserve complete thoughts around cuts. Subtitles match speech; marketing callouts are distinct. Silent films still need visible context/action/result/CTA.

Check "all", "instant", "secure", superlatives, numerical improvements and offers against exact evidence. Sample counts describe the fixture only. Do not borrow customers or traction. Label deterministic AI samples and preserve actual authority boundaries.

Give the result reading time and end once. A loop may return to its initial state rather than use a sales outro; match image/cursor/movement at the seam. A standalone ad needs a legible close. Derive duration from proof and constraints, not a fixed template.
