## Skill catalog

- [pds-audio-captions](../skills/pds-audio-captions/SKILL.md): Use when aligning supplied narration and exact captions, planning optional licensed music or sound cues, measuring audio, and preserving muted comprehension in product video.
- [pds-campaign](../skills/pds-campaign/SKILL.md): Use when defining a product-video campaign angle, audience, awareness stage, placement, CTA or bounded paid-social matrix with genuinely different test hypotheses.
- [pds-delivery](../skills/pds-delivery/SKILL.md): Use when packaging final product videos, editable sources, posters, captions, campaign copy, experiment manifests, provenance and QA for sharing or website integration.
- [pds-hooks-and-script](../skills/pds-hooks-and-script/SKILL.md): Use when writing distinct openings and causal scripts for a short product demo, launch video, feature announcement, paid ad, narrated walkthrough or product-led promotional film.
- [pds-product-evidence](../skills/pds-product-evidence/SKILL.md): Use when selecting and capturing a defensible product workflow for video, tracing claims/assets, or preparing a faithful replay from product components and verified interface states.
- [pds-render](../skills/pds-render/SKILL.md): Use when selecting an explicit product-video renderer, producing previews/final exports, compositing real footage, or reproducing a render with source and configuration hashes.
- [pds-review](../skills/pds-review/SKILL.md): Use when checking and repairing an encoded product demo's technical quality, evidence timing, UI readability, motion, captions, audio or editorial effectiveness before delivery.
- [pds-storyboard](../skills/pds-storyboard/SKILL.md): Use when planning product-video shots, source assets, styleframes, pacing, narration cues, transitions and aspect-ratio compositions, or selecting reusable product shot recipes.
- [pds-ui-motion](../skills/pds-ui-motion/SKILL.md): Use when reconstructing verified product UI for controlled animation or implementing deterministic frame motion, cursor choreography, springs, camera focus and connected transformations in product video.
- [product-demo-studio](../skills/product-demo-studio/SKILL.md): Use when producing a product demo video, organic launch clip, paid social creative, feature announcement, verified walkthrough or homepage loop that shows meaningful product behavior. Also use for purposeful campaign variants; do not activate for a live marketing site or unrelated video editing.
