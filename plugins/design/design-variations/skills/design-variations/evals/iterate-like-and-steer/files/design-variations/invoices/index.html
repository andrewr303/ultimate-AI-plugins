Turn 1 · Invoices list

Ten ways to find what needs chasing

Every option shows the same 18 invoices with today's totals and keeps every function on the page: status filters with counts, search, five sorts, bulk reminders and mark paid, the row menu, Export CSV and New invoice with its checks. What varies is what leads and how you get to the invoices that need chasing, from a re-weighted table to a one-at-a-time chase queue. Every frame is live: filter, sort, select, open menus and the New invoice panel; use the State menu for long values or no invoices. Reminders, export and navigation are simulated with a confirmation.
