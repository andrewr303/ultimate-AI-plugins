Four ways to work through a reading list

Every option uses the same four articles and keeps saving a link, marking an article read, and the All, Unread and Read filter. Every frame is live. List layout in Tweaks on Example-base switches between today, 1a, 1b and 1c. (A real round holds ten options, 1a to 1j, one per rung.)

Now Today The reading list as it ships, unchanged, for comparison.
1a Calmer rows One soft panel instead of boxed rows, with reading times in an aligned right column. Barely changes anything people rely on.
1b Reading-time chips Reading times become filled chips, warm for quick reads. Adds colour that competes with the Save button.
1c Time first Two-line rows that lead with how long each article takes, then the title. Fewer articles fit on screen.
1d Reading-time lanes Articles sit in Quick reads, This weekend and Long reads lanes, with filters kept and new links landing in Quick reads. A new layout to learn, and it needs width.
